Who We Are

Bridging Real Estate Deals with Uncompromising Legal Diligence

Tricore Realty & Advisory is a premier real estate brokerage and revenue legal consultancy headquartered in Maharashtra. We combine deep property advisory expertise with in-house government revenue record due diligence.

Our Core Pillars

Built on a foundation of legal clarity, absolute transparency, and market foresight.

100% Legal Due Diligence

Every property listed with Tricore undergoes comprehensive 30 to 100-year title searches, revenue register (7/12 & CTS) verifications, and encumbrance checks prior to marketing.

Zero-Dispute Guarantee

We safeguard buyers and land investors from litigated properties, family partition disputes, or unauthorized land classifications through direct government revenue desk clearance.

End-to-End Registration

From price negotiations and token agreements to Sub-Registrar stamp duty registration, home loan processing, and final 7/12 or CTS mutation entries, we manage every step.
